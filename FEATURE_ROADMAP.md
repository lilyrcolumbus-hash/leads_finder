## Features To Implement (Priority Order)

### 1. Lead Warming System (HIGHEST PRIORITY)
**Impact: +300-500% Conversion Rate**

The process of "warming" leads before cold contact significantly increases response rates.

#### Components:
- **LinkedIn Profile Viewer**: Automatically view prospect profiles so they see your visit
- **LinkedIn Engagement Tracker**: Track and engage with prospect content
- **Smart Comment Generator**: AI-generated relevant comments for prospect posts
- **Connection Request Manager**: Send personalized connection requests
- **Warming Calendar**: Visual calendar showing warming activities
- **Lead Temperature Score**: Track how "warm" each lead is (Cold → Warm → Hot)
- **"Ready to Contact" Alert**: Notification when lead is sufficiently warmed

#### Warming Process Flow:
```
Day 1: View LinkedIn Profile
Day 2: Like 2-3 of their posts
Day 3: Comment on a relevant post
Day 4: Send connection request
Day 5-7: Engage with their content
Day 8+: Send personalized email (they already know you!)
```

## Why Lead Warming First?

1. **Highest ROI**: Converting cold outreach to warm outreach has the biggest impact
2. **Differentiator**: Most competitors skip this step
3. **Foundation**: Warmed leads respond better to all other features
4. **No Additional Cost**: Uses existing LinkedIn (no API fees)
